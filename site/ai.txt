# ai.txt — AI & content-usage policy for Cairn
# https://cairn.dosanjhlabs.com

This file is both human- and machine-readable. It states how automated and
AI-related systems may use the content published at cairn.dosanjhlabs.com.

## Answer engines & AI search (welcomed)

AI answer engines, AI-powered search, and assistants that crawl this site to
answer a user's question and cite/link back to Cairn as the source are
welcome to crawl this site. This includes (non-exhaustively) OAI-SearchBot,
ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User,
Applebot, Amazon's search agents, Meta's web indexer/fetcher, MistralAI's
index/user agents, and DuckAssistBot. See robots.txt for the authoritative
per-bot rules.

## AI / ML training (not permitted without a license)

Automated collection of this site's content for the purpose of training or
fine-tuning AI/ML models, building a RAG corpus, or otherwise constructing a
dataset for model training is NOT permitted without a separate license. This
applies to crawlers such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, Claude-Web,
Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, Meta-ExternalAgent, CCBot, Bytespider,
and similar dataset/training crawlers — see robots.txt for the full,
authoritative list.

All content on this site is reserved under the W3C TDM Reservation Protocol
(TDMRep) — see /.well-known/tdmrep.json and /ai-policy.html.

## Product

Cairn is a free, open-source (AGPL-3.0) tool that reconciles device inventory
from MDM/EDR sources (Jamf, Intune, Kandji, JumpCloud, CrowdStrike, Sophos,
Defender, and more) into Snipe-IT as a single system of record, with a
read-only drift report showing where the CMDB disagrees with reality.
Source: https://github.com/jsdosanj/cairn
