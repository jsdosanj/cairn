AI & content-usage policy

Answer and search AI is welcome to crawl this site and cite Cairn in its responses — that's traffic we want. Automated collection of this content for AI/ML training, fine-tuning, or dataset construction is reserved and not permitted without a license.

Answer & search AI: welcome

AI-powered search and assistants — OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User, Applebot, Amazon's search agents, Meta's web indexer/fetcher, MistralAI's index/user agents, and DuckAssistBot — may crawl this site freely to answer questions and cite Cairn as the source. See robots.txt for the authoritative per-bot rules.

AI / ML training: reserved

Automated collection of this site's content to train or fine-tune AI or machine-learning models, to build a retrieval (RAG) corpus, or to otherwise construct a dataset is not permitted without a license. Known training and dataset crawlers — GPTBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, Claude-Web, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, Meta-ExternalAgent, FacebookBot, Amazonbot, CCBot, Bytespider, cohere-ai, and similar — are disallowed in robots.txt. Google-Extended and Applebot-Extended are training-only opt-out tokens; blocking them does not affect regular Googlebot or Applebot search indexing.

How this is enforced

This policy is expressed in layered, machine-readable form so both AI crawlers and standards-aware tooling can honor it automatically:

robots.txt

Explicit per-bot Allow rules for answer/search AI and Disallow rules for training/dataset crawlers.

TDM Reservation Protocol

A machine-readable reservation at /.well-known/tdmrep.json, per the W3C TDMRep standard.

ai.txt

A human- and machine-readable summary of this policy at /ai.txt.

HTTP response headers

Every page is served with X-Robots-Tag: noai, noimageai, Content-Usage: train-ai=n, search=y, TDM-Reservation: 1, and a TDM-Policy link back to this page — while search indexing stays fully enabled.

Frequently asked questions

Can AI answer engines and assistants crawl this site?

Yes. Answer and search-oriented AI — including OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User, Applebot, Amazon's search agents, Meta's web indexer/fetcher, MistralAI's index/user agents, and DuckAssistBot — is welcome to crawl this site and cite it in answers. That kind of crawling drives traffic back to Cairn and we want it.

Can this content be used to train AI or machine-learning models?

Not without a license. Automated collection of this site's content for AI/ML training, fine-tuning, building a RAG corpus, or otherwise constructing a dataset is not permitted. This content is reserved under the W3C TDM (Text and Data Mining) Reservation Protocol.

Which crawlers are allowed, and which are blocked?

robots.txt is the authoritative list. Answer/search crawlers are explicitly allowed. Known AI training and dataset crawlers — including GPTBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, Claude-Web, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, Meta-ExternalAgent, CCBot, Bytespider, and similar — are disallowed.

Does blocking Google-Extended or Applebot-Extended hurt my search ranking?

No. Google-Extended and Applebot-Extended are training-only opt-out control tokens. Disallowing them stops Google's and Apple's AI-training use of this content but has no effect on regular Googlebot or Applebot search indexing, which stays fully allowed.

How is this policy technically enforced?

Through layered, machine-readable signals: per-bot Allow/Disallow rules in robots.txt, a TDM reservation record at /.well-known/tdmrep.json, an /ai.txt policy file, and HTTP response headers (X-Robots-Tag: noai, noimageai; Content-Usage: train-ai=n, search=y; TDM-Reservation: 1; TDM-Policy) sent with every page.

How do I license this content for AI training, or ask a question about this policy?

Get in touch through the contact form. We're open to licensing conversations for AI/ML training or dataset use — just not to having it taken without one.
